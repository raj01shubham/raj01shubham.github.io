## Why I'm starting this blog

I'm an automation test engineer with 3.5 years of experience, and I've decided to transition into cybersecurity. This blog is where I'll document that journey publicly — what I learn, what I get wrong, and what works.

## What I'm going to do

Over the next ~13 months, I'm working toward a role as a Cloud Security Analyst or SOC Analyst with an AWS focus. The plan looks roughly like this:

- **Phase 1 (now):** Foundations — networking, Linux, Python for security
- **Phase 2:** Core security skills + CompTIA Security+ certification
- **Phase 3:** AWS Security Specialty certification
- **Phase 4:** Pivot — internal at my current company (Plan A) or external (Plan B)
- **Phase 5 onwards:** Deeper specialization in DFIR, malware analysis, and AI/ML security

## Why public?

Two reasons:
1. **Accountability.** Writing publicly means I can't quietly give up.
2. **Portfolio.** In the Indian cybersecurity job market, a public learning record is one of the strongest signals you can give a recruiter.

## What's next

This week I'm starting with TryHackMe's Pre-Security path and a Python-for-security refresher. I'll write up what I learn as I go.

If you're on a similar path, I'd love to hear from you.
